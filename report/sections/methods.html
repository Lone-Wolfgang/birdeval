2.2 Dataset: BirdSet

{{block:site_table}}
  • Site biomes are WWF biomes assigned per site ({{file:configs/reference/sites.csv}}); the sites are small areas, so a label describes the site's setting, not every recorder.
  • POW is BirdSet's validation set, not a test set. It is included here like the others.
  • Calling times and call frequency bands come from BirdSet's call-level annotations ({{file:scripts/site_calls.py}}). BirdSet's “local_time” is UTC for PER and SSW (their file names end in Z); those are converted to local time. Most sites record only around dawn (PER 05–06 h, POW and HSN about 05–09 h); SSW records all day and UHH 05–23 h, so only those two show full daily patterns. Activity is calls per recorded hour, and unrecorded hours are shown as unknown. NBP has no times or frequency boxes.
  • Body mass, lifestyle and habitat are AVONET; diet, foraging stratum and the nocturnal flag are EltonTraits 1.0, matched to eBird names through AVONET's crosswalk. Xeno-canto counts were queried on {{xc_query_date}}: they are today's counts, larger than what any model trained on, and xeno-canto's IOC names can split or lump differently from eBird's.

  • Species names follow the 2025 eBird taxonomy. Older model names are mapped through {{file:configs/taxonomy/synonyms.yaml}}. Every species at every site is scored for every model, including the 4 species BirdNET 2.4 cannot predict, which count against it.

2.5 Evaluation criteria

cmAP
Average precision per species, averaged over species. Treats rare and common species equally.
AUROC
Chance a positive segment scores higher than a negative one, averaged over species.
Top-1
Share of segments with at least one label where the top-scoring species is one of them.
Recording cmAP
cmAP for species presence per recording unit, from soundscape mode.
Recall @90%P
At the single score threshold where 90% of a model's claims are correct, the share of true presences it finds. Per clip in clips mode, per recording unit in soundscape mode.
Positives
Labelled 5-second segments with the species at that site.
